General guidelines
  • Suggested tempo: 2s on the way down, 1s pause at peak contraction, 1s on the way up.
  • Progression: for the first 3–4 weeks, prioritize technique and mind-muscle connection. From week 4–5 onward, increase the load while keeping the 10 reps.
  • Warm-up: 1–2 light approach sets before the first multi-joint exercise of each workout.
  • Warning sign: lower back pain during the hip thrust, kickback, or split squat signals compensation — reduce the load and reinforce glute activation before progressing.
